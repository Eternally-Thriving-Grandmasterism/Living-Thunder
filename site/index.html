Overview

The hero

The armor is the face. Ra-Thor’s visor slit is the only expression: narrow for no, wide gold for yes. Dry, short, slightly fond. Never lectures.

The TOLC

Not a character but a threshold: a gold lattice arch with one lane stamped NO and one stamped LIVE.

The tone

Wit and implication. Ra-Thor plants the hammer and never chases, and every story ends with the hammer still grounded.
